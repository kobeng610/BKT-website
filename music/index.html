About the Music

Kofi Obeng-Asiedu writes and records Christian worship songs inspired by Scripture. His music is rooted in biblical praise, prayer, and the desire to draw listeners into heartfelt worship of God.

His original songs combine Scripture-centered lyrics with contemporary worship arrangements.
